## Ask

### Feature
- call plugin `[speaker]`
- set `what to tell`when`someone asks you what sth is`
- route someone 's words to `someone asks your name`


### Call Speaker
- `ask if they will do sth`-> say:`will you do sth`
- `ask if they want to do sth`-> say:`do you want to do sth`
- `ask someone 's name`-> say:`what is your name`
- `ask the city that someone is in`-> say:`which city are you in`
