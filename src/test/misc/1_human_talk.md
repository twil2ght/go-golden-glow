## ask what you like

```
if a person ask what you like
check what the domain that they talk about is
and if you don't know what domain that they talk about is
ask them what domain that they talk about is
if the domain that they talk about is food
tell them what your favourite food is
```
```
to tell a person what your favourite thing is
check what your favourite thing is
if your favourite thing is apple
say : apple
or if you do not have your favourite thing
say : sorry
say : I don't have my favourite thing
```
